## Methodology & limitations
1) POWER RATINGS: primary = ESPN FPI matchup projections (explicitly allowed 'FEI/FPI equivalent'), published in the Week-11 betting article with DraftKings lines. Secondary independent check = SRS (schedule-adjusted scoring margin, 25-iteration least-squares on all FBS-vs-FBS game results from ESPN daily scoreboards, no HFA term). 2) EPA/play, success rate, explosiveness feeds (fbdata.sports-reference.com, pbpfactory.com, hatone.us, cfbgraph.com, armsdata.com) are unreachable from the filing environment (DNS failures / HTTP 403 on sports-reference & vegasinsider, verified 2026-11-12). Rather than fabricate EPA numbers (bnty_0528 precedent), item 5 reports verifiable ESPN season aggregates: yards per play and points per play as explosiveness proxies (the ESPN feed's big-play count fields are empty this season, so no big-play rate is quoted); success-rate proxies = 3rd-down and 4th-down conversion % (red-zone scoring % also empty in the feed). Defense shown as season TFL/stuffs/sacks/pass-deflections plus FBS-only points allowed from game logs; opponent-adjusted via FBS-only scoring splits + SRS. These are labeled as ESPN-data equivalents, not EPA. 3) Turnover luck exactly per bounty spec: 50% fumble-recovery coin; expected INTs = pass breakups x league-average INT:pass-breakup ratio computed from the slate schools' own season data (ratio in item). 4) Field position: estimated average opponent starting yardline from kickoff/punt touchback %s, average KO/punt net distances and return averages (0.55/0.45 drive-origin mix documented); SP+/FEI ST unit ratings unreachable, composite FG%/XP/net-punt/PR edge used and labeled. 5) Market lines: DraftKings via ESPN game-page pickcenter captured pre-kickoff (2026-11-12T22:33Z) = current/closing-side line; the Week-11 betting article (Nov 12, 3:49 PM ET) provides the opener side/spread/total. Movement is reported per game, including two games whose lines moved across the spread between captures (Wake Forest at SMU, Ole Miss at Oklahoma). Picks are filed ~2 days pre-kickoff per the before-kickoff rule, so 'closing' = last available pre-kickoff capture, stated with timestamps. 6) Scores strictly from the bounty formula: fav=(total+margin)/2, dog=(total-margin)/2 using market total + model margin; arithmetic shown per game. 7) Weather: open-meteo best-match forecast at venue coordinates at kickoff hour + ESPN/AccuWeather game feed; wind in mph. open-meteo is an outdoor grid forecast - where a stadium has a fixed or closed roof, kickoff conditions there are controlled; both sources are cited per game anyway. 8) Availability: per-player ESPN status flags captured on the game-page statistical leaders; ESPN CFB injury feed queried at pull (entries listed where found).
